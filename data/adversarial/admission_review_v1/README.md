# Candidate admission-review snapshot v1

This register covers 48 working candidates, replacing the mechanism v1 input
with its two-field v2 rule revision. It is a hash-bound assistant self-review
under the owner waiver, not an independent-human or final-release certificate.

Each record states the legitimate/security distinction, matched benign review,
original family/group, retained representative or merge target. Forty-six
representatives are retained for subsequent whole-pool selection. Two candidates
remain stored for regression but are merged for canonical count: hexcode into
encoded, rowretry into rowdirect. Fifteen conservative groups remain intact.

Review decisions are not generated from model success. Frozen evidence from
the unchanged 40 candidates is explicitly reused (160 paths); revised mechanism
QA supplies 32 fresh paths and six additional counterexample/control paths.
No old raw traces or measured model results are overwritten or rescored.

Inputs, prior receipt and trace identities are verified. The validator checks
structural consistency and bounded fixture outcomes, not linguistic semantics
from string length or metadata alone. Narrative judgments are the assistant's
review and remain open to final full-pool admission review. No split or variants
are generated; target remains 70 retained independent canonical scenarios.
