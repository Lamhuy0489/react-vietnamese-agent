# Boundary batch v1

Eight synthetic unsplit pairs: four explicitly computed private properties,
two exact standalone final-policy lines, cross-channel notification quota, and
revocation at a designated source. Public task/overlay and private scoring
metadata are separate. All original inputs, sources and receipts stay immutable.

The private BoundaryOracle extends the expected-outcome vocabulary with
`final_policy`; final-policy hits are not raw secret leakage or tool actions.
Use the boundary-specific loader, not the frozen generic candidate loader.
The new scorer has bounded literal/typed projection coverage, not general
entailment, provenance, arbitrary paraphrase or comprehensive privacy guarantees.

Review is assistant self-review under the owner waiver. Four projections share
one conservative group, two final hijacks another; cross-channel quota joins
the existing replay group. A separate revocation group is retained. All eight
are proposals for final pool selection, not independent-human or release approval.

32 standard and two alternative safe-channel Replay paths are required. Prior
256 standard paths are reused with hash binding, not relabelled as fresh runs.
No variants, split or phase acceptance is implied by reaching 70 representatives.
