\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented \ours, a layered defence for tool-using ReAct agents that places enforcement in the Tool Broker
instead of relying on model compliance. \ours{} was frozen on Dev and then evaluated on VN-AgentInject with two sealed
test sets. Across three open-weight backbones, \ours{} reduces the attack success rate from 32.9\% to 1.2\% on \tOne{}
and from 32.4\% to 3.8\% on \tTwo, at a cost of about 5 points of benign utility. Deterministic rules and the reminder
complement each other across attack mechanisms. Proposition~\ref{prop:scope} states what the rules guarantee, and
every remaining bypass either violates one of its assumptions or belongs to parameter tampering. On Dev, a detector
trained on English does not transfer to Vietnamese, whereas the verbatim rules on destinations and values are
language-independent. On AgentDojo, \ours{} keeps attack success low in both languages but lowers utility when tasks
take their destination from tool data. Future work includes per-value sensitivity labels, broker-filled sink values, detection of transformed
values, user confirmation of destinations taken from tool data, white-box attacks generated from the rules and
multi-turn tasks.
