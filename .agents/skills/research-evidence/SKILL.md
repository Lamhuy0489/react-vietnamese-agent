# Research Evidence

Start with a specific research question or thesis claim. Prefer peer-reviewed
papers, original technical reports, official documentation, and primary
datasets. Record title, authors/organization, year, stable URL or DOI, access
date when appropriate, supported claim, limitations, and whether the note is
direct evidence or an inference.

Separate findings from recommendations and identify conflicting evidence.
Avoid citation laundering through blogs when the primary source is available.
Quote sparingly and preserve enough bibliographic metadata for the thesis.

Map each proposed thesis statement to evidence and flag unsupported or
overbroad claims. New research does not authorize changing the frozen project
scope or architecture; propose such a change in
`../../../docs/project/decision_log.md` and wait for explicit approval.
